Request form · prototype

Apply for re-admission

Enter the information needed for a re-admission request. Please do not enter sensitive personal details in this demo version.

Rs. 700Re-admission fee (as supplied for this draft)Confirm the current amount and payment instructions with the college office before using this as an official portal. This website does not take online payments.

Prototype only: this form does not send an application, change student status, or record payment at the college.

How it works

Re-admission steps

01
Fill in student details

Use your college roll number and select First Year or Second Year.

02
Explain the situation

Choose the reason and provide a brief, respectful explanation.

03
Confirm the fee

The draft shows Rs. 700 based on the information supplied. Verify the amount with the authorized college office.

04
Office review

The college administration decides whether re-admission can be approved and confirms any required documents.

Important

Only authorized college staff can confirm strike-off status, re-admission eligibility, official fees and approval. A teacher or website form does not itself grant re-admission.
